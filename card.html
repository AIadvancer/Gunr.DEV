Print tips

Get the size right

  • Paper: US Letter. Scale: 100% / "Actual size", never "Fit to page".
  • Margins: None (or Default in Chrome). The sheet sets its own margins.
  • Dark look: tick Background graphics in the print dialog.
  • Duplex: flip on the long edge. Print one test sheet, hold it up to a light, then nudge the back if needed.
  • Stock: 14pt or 16pt matte cardstock. Matte avoids glare, so codes scan better. Check your printer's max paper weight.
  • No heavy-stock printer? Send the SVG to a print shop. It stays sharp at any size.
  • Before printing a batch, test-scan with two different phones: the poster from 1 m (3 ft) and a card at arm's length.

Networking in the field

  • NFC tag on your phone case: stick an NTAG213/215 sticker on the back and write your URL to it with a free NFC writer app. Test it, then lock the tag. People can tap your phone to open the site.
  • Lock-screen QR: set this as your wallpaper. It fits iPhone-size screens and works on most phones. Raise your phone and they can scan it, no unlocking needed.
  • Put a sticker on the back of your conference badge and your laptop lid.
  • Save your own .vcf to your phone so you can AirDrop or share it in a couple of taps.
  • Put the same URL in your email signature and LinkedIn header, so every channel leads to one place.
